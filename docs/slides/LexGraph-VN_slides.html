Motivation

Correct article, wrong answer

"Xe máy vượt đèn đỏ bị phạt bao nhiêu tiền?"

  • Vietnamese law changes constantly: laws are amended, decrees replaced, circulars repealed.
  • A retriever that returns a repealed article is confidently wrong, yet standard metrics count it as a hit.
  • Legal texts form a network: Laws are guided by Decrees and Circulars and cite each other.
31.3%

of the QA passages we linked to the national legal portal come from documents that are no longer in force.

Another 32.6% come from documents that are partly expired.

Problem

Answer under the law in force at question time

Given a question $q$ asked at time $t_q$, return the articles that answer it under the law in force at $t_q$, and a short cited answer.

RQ1 · Time

How often are retrieved articles outdated, and how much do validity signals fix it?

RQ2 · Relations

Which relation types (amends, replaces / repeals) matter for finding the current law?

RQ3 · Answers

Can a small self-hosted LLM (Qwen3-4B) cite correctly and explain legal changes?

Approach

Legal QA as a web mining problem

Content mining

Parse HTML into articles (Điều); extract cross-references; link QA passages to their source article.

Structure mining

Typed relation graph (amends, replaces, repeals, guides, cites); validity of each document from its links; Louvain communities.

Usage for search

Validity intervals and version alignment re-rank results; an LLM writes the answer with citations.

Knowledge graph · construction

From portal pages to a typed graph

  • Relations are listed from each document's own page, so direction depends on the label.
  • We map 26 labels → 9 typed edges actor → target, and check each direction against issue dates: the actor is newer in 96.0–100% of edges per label.
  • HTML → 981,412 articles from 127,802 documents.
  • Validity interval per document: effective date → expiry date, or the date of the first repealing document.
  • The links decide validity: an article never says in its own text that it was superseded; only the amends / replaces / repeals links of other documents do.

Temporal layer

Which version of an article is in force?

Nghị định 100/2019/NĐ-CPin force 01/01/2020
← amends
Nghị định 123/2021/NĐ-CPin force 01/01/2022
← amends
Nghị định 168/2024/NĐ-CPin force 01/01/2025
  • For a passage in document $D$, look for successor articles in documents that amend, replace or repeal $D$ (up to 2 hops).
  • Version alignment: a gradient-boosting classifier on pair features (similarity, cross-encoder score, heading and token overlap, article distance, relation type, clause-level amendment), trained on 2,359 pairs judged by gpt-oss-120b, then one-to-one matching per document pair.
  • Successor articles are chunked by clause and added to the corpus, so the current law can be retrieved.
  • At query time: stale = document not in force at $t_q$, or article superseded by a successor in force.

System

Pipeline

Offline (web mining)

vbpl.vn crawl→ Articles, cross-references, relation canonicalisation→ Typed KG · link analysis→ Validity intervals, version alignment, clause-level amendments→ Incremental sitemap crawl

Online (question answering)

Query normalisation→BGE-M3 (fine-tuned) top-100→ Inject successors of stale candidates→ BGE-Reranker-v2-M3 (fine-tuned) top-30→ Stale penalty at $t_q$→ Qwen3-4B (vLLM)→ Streamlit
all components run end to end on our server · bi-encoder and reranker fine-tuned
Method

Time-aware re-ranking

$$s(q,d) = \underbrace{s_{\mathrm{CE}}(q,d)}_{\text{semantic}} - \gamma\,\underbrace{\mathbf{1}\left[d \text{ not in force at } t_q\right]}_{\text{stale penalty}}$$
  • Successor injection: the successors of stale candidates join the cross-encoder candidates, so the current version is found even when the retriever only sees the old one.
  • Everyday wording is mapped to legal wording first ("vượt đèn đỏ" → "không chấp hành hiệu lệnh của đèn tín hiệu giao thông").
  • $\gamma$ tuned on validation by MRR@10 against the current gold ($\gamma = 2$).
  • New metric Stale@k: share of the top-$k$ results not in force at question time.
Answer generation

Self-hosted Qwen3-4B, grounded on valid articles

  • Served with vLLM on our own GPU; no external LLM API.
  • Context: the most relevant clause of each article in force, the old version of a changed article and the amendments of the clauses shown.
  • Verified change facts: a notice is allowed only when the clause asked about really differs between old and new version (fine amounts or wording); a post-generation check removes unsupported notices.
  • Non-thinking mode by default; thinking mode as an option.

Rules in the prompt

  1. Answer only from the given articles.
  2. Cite each claim as [k] with document and article.
  3. Prefer articles in force; add a "⚠️ Thay đổi pháp luật" section only from the verified changes.
  4. Say "Không tìm thấy đủ căn cứ pháp lý" when evidence is missing.

Results · "law changed" notices (validation, 300 stable + 150 changed questions)

Only verified changes become a notice

  1. Take the most relevant stale article and its aligned successor.
  2. Compare the clause the question is about: the old rule just before the change (phrase replacements applied) vs. the new clause.
  3. Keep the fact if the fine amounts differ or token Jaccard < 0.85.
  4. Qwen3-4B answers one word: does the answer to this question differ?
  5. After generation, a notice without a verified fact is removed; a missing one is added.

Discussion

What we learned, what comes next

Lessons and limitations

  • Validity matters: 31% of linked gold passages are outdated; validity signals cut outdated results in the top 10 by almost half.
  • Too much penalty hurts: beyond γ = 2 the system also hides useful context.
  • A 4B model trips on cross-references inside an article ("trừ … điểm e khoản 4"), even with the right clause in context.
  • 35% of QA passages are not linked to the portal, so their validity is unknown.

Next steps

  • Old versions as hard negatives (temporal contrastive training).
  • Higher recall of version alignment (one-to-one matching keeps only the safest links).
  • Claim-level citation checking (NLI) against the cited clause.
  • Multi-hop Law → Decree → Circular answers; user study.
